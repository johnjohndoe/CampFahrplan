The 3rd Berlin FediDay is a networking event for people in the Fediverse.
Through talks, workshops and networking opportunities, we aim to spread
knowledge about the Fediverse, promote creative solutions and strengthen
dialogue between developers, admins, academia, civil society and content
creators.

With over 70 sessions across three halls, a workshop room and several
cosy meeting spots stretching from the banks of the Spree to the roof
terrace, FediDay is the largest conference dedicated to alternative social
networks. It will take place from 11 to 13 September at c-base in Berlin.

🔍 https://berlinfedi.day

<b>Features</b>
* View program by day and rooms (side by side)
* Custom grid layout for smartphones (rotate your device 🙃) and tablets
* Read detailed descriptions (speaker names, start time, room name, links, ...) of sessions
* Search and filter through all sessions
* Add sessions to favorites list
* Export favorites list
* Setup alarms for individual sessions
* Add sessions to your personal calendar
* Share a website link to a session with others
* Keep track of program changes
* Automatic program updates (configurable in settings)
* Integration with the Engelsystem project https://engelsystem.anoxinon.de - Online tool for coordinating helpers and shifts on large events
* Integration with Chaosflix https://github.com/NiciDieNase/chaosflix - Android App for https://media.ccc.de, share schedule favorites with Chaosflix and import them as bookmarks (RIP NiciDieNase)
